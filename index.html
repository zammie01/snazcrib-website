Questions people actually ask

Is SnazCrib really free for tenants?

Yes. Searching, chatting with landlords, and booking inspections cost nothing. You only pay the rent itself, through escrow, once you're ready to move in.

What does "LUC verified" actually mean?

Landlords upload their Land Use Charge document — proof of property tax payment tied to the property. We run it through OCR and match the name on the document against the landlord's account. If it matches, the listing carries a verified badge.

What happens to my rent money before I move in?

It sits in escrow, not the landlord's account. It's only released once both you and the landlord confirm the handover happened — if something's wrong, it stays held until it's resolved.

Can I still get scammed by a fake landlord?

We can't promise zero risk in any marketplace, but LUC verification, in-app agreements, and escrow together remove the three points where most Nigerian rental scams happen: fake ownership, undocumented agreements, and blind cash handover.
